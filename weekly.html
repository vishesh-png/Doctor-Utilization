Definitions — everything is measured in minutes of roster capacity, because SH and MH consults have different lengths (see config chips below). Gross utilization = (completed + no-show time) ÷ total slot time. Net utilization = completed time ÷ total slot time. Rescheduled and cancelled bookings are shown separately — they release the slot back, so they don't count as utilization. Program: bookings tagged mental_health → MH; sexual_health or untagged → SH (verified: all untagged bookings have SH-config durations). Statuses: completed = COMPLETED, no-show = MISSED, rescheduled = RESCHEDULED.

Screening Calls (SC)

The roster is cut into SH-sized SC units; one MH screening consumes multiple consecutive units.

Repeats (FU · Report Reading · Patient Queries)
