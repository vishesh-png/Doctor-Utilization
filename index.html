Doctor Utilization

Gross utilization = share of a doctor's net bookable slot time consumed by a patient who showed up or no-showed. Net utilization = share consumed by completed consults only. Denominator is realized roster slot time after shrinkage (booked + still-bookable), from roster_slots. SC = Screening Calls · FU = repeat types (Follow Up / Report Reading / Patient Queries).
